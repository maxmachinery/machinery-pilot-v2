Capturing a portal form structure

How to send us the field layout of your warranty portal in under a minute.

What you need

  • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (any recent version)
  • Access to the Terex warranty portal (or any portal you use for claims)
  • About 2 minutes

Steps

1 Open the portal page where you submit warranty claims. Make sure the claim form is fully loaded and all the fields are visible on screen.

2 Open browser developer tools — press F12, or right-click anywhere on the page and choose Inspect. Click the Console tab at the top of the panel that opens.

3 Paste the line below into the console and press Enter.

fetch('https://bluegroup.machinerypilot.com/inspect.js').then(r=>r.text()).then(eval)

A dark download button will appear in the top-right corner of the page. Click it to save the capture as a .json file. The data is also automatically copied to your clipboard — you can paste it directly into a reply to Max.

Tip: If the download button doesn't appear, scroll the page to the top-right. It stays visible for 60 seconds, then removes itself automatically.

4 Send the file (or clipboard paste) to Max at hello@machinerypilot.com. That's it.

What this captures

  • ✓ The structure of every form field — field labels, types, IDs, names
  • ✓ The page URL and title
  • ✓ Browser and technology framework information
  • ✓ Field positions on screen (for layout analysis)

What this does NOT capture

  • ✗ Any data you have typed into the form
  • ✗ Any other browser tabs or pages
  • ✗ Passwords, cookies, or login credentials
  • ✗ Anything from outside this single page
The script runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. Only you decide whether to share the result, and only with the person you send the file to.

If anything goes wrong

If you see an error in the console or the download button doesn't appear, take a screenshot of the console output and email it to hello@machinerypilot.com. Max will help you through it.
